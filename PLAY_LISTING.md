## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

Immerse yourself in ZenFlow — a sleek, soothing offline color connect and pipe flow puzzle designed to relax your mind and sharpen your spatial reasoning.

Connect matching pairs of vibrant color dots by drawing fluid, glowing pipes across the grid. Every path must connect seamlessly without crossing or overlapping other colors. To earn the coveted 3-star rating, connect all pairs AND cover 100% of the board!

Whether you have two minutes while waiting in line or an hour to unwind before sleep, ZenFlow delivers a peaceful, distraction-free gaming experience with zero ads, zero trackers, and complete offline capability.

### 🌟 Game Features:

- **100% Offline Gaming**: Play anywhere, anytime — on airplanes, subways, or during road trips without requiring Wi-Fi or cellular data.
- **50 Carefully Crafted Levels**: Explore 5 progressive difficulty packs designed for all skill levels:
  • Starter 5x5: Gentle warmups to master the flow mechanics
  • Classic 6x6: Balanced puzzles with increasing color combinations
  • Challenger 7x7: Intricate layouts demanding forward planning
  • Expert 8x8: Complex multi-color mazes for seasoned puzzle solvers
  • Master 9x9: Expansive brain-teasers that truly test your mastery
- **Harmonic Web Audio Synthesizer**: Delight in procedural pentatonic chimes tuned to harmonic frequencies for each unique color. Every connection produces a satisfying musical tone.
- **Colorblind Friendly Mode**: Toggle clear letter identifiers inside each color node with a single tap in the settings for accessible, inclusive gameplay.
- **Intelligent Hint System**: Stuck on a tricky layout? Use the hint button to uncover the exact path for any unsolved color.
- **Unlimited Undo & Instant Reset**: Experiment freely with infinite move take-backs and quick board resets.
- **Sleek Minimalist Dark Aesthetic**: Beautiful neon glow paths against a battery-saving dark slate backdrop.
- **Full Privacy Guarantee**: No permissions, no personal data collection, no background tracking. Your game stays on your device.
